# Privacy Policy for Weather From Hell

**Last updated: August 10, 2026**

Weather From Hell is a weather application developed and published by **Euphoria Creations**.

This Privacy Policy explains how Weather From Hell accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the application.

This policy applies to both **Weather From Hell** and **Weather From Hell Free**. Features involving advertising apply only to Weather From Hell Free.

## Location Information

Weather From Hell may request access to your device's location in order to determine your current location and provide weather information relevant to that location.

Location information is used to obtain weather conditions and forecasts requested by the user.

Users may also choose a location manually rather than relying on the device's current location.

Weather From Hell does not use location information for continuous background tracking.

Location permissions can be changed or revoked at any time through the Android device settings.

## Weather Data

Weather From Hell communicates with third-party weather and location services in order to retrieve weather information and convert location information into human-readable place names.

Information necessary to perform these requests, such as geographic coordinates or a manually selected location, may be transmitted to these services.

This information is used to provide the weather functionality requested by the user.

## Local App Data

Weather From Hell stores certain information locally on the user's device so that application features continue to work between sessions.

This may include:

- Application settings and preferences
- Selected or previously used location information
- Achievement progress and unlocked achievements
- Notification preferences
- Other application state required for Weather From Hell features

This locally stored application data is not used by Euphoria Creations to create a user account or personal profile.

Weather From Hell does not currently require users to create an account.

Locally stored application data may be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.

## Notifications

If the user enables notifications, Weather From Hell may schedule and display weather-related notifications on the device.

Notification preferences and information necessary to schedule these notifications may be stored locally on the device.

Users can disable notification permissions through Android settings.

## Advertising — Weather From Hell Free

**Weather From Hell Free** contains advertising provided through Google AdMob.

The paid version of **Weather From Hell does not contain AdMob advertising.**

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information for purposes including advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Depending on the user's device, region, privacy choices, and Google's services, this information may include:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
- Device and advertising identifiers
- App and advertisement interactions
- Diagnostic and performance information

Advertising data is processed by Google according to Google's own privacy policies and applicable privacy choices.

Where required by applicable law, Weather From Hell Free uses Google's consent tools to request or manage advertising-related privacy choices before requesting advertisements.

Users in applicable regions may be provided options to consent, decline consent, manage their choices, or change their privacy choices later.

For more information about how Google handles information used for advertising, please see Google's Privacy & Terms documentation.

## Third-Party Services

Weather From Hell may use third-party services necessary to provide application functionality.

These may include:

- Weather data services
- Geocoding or location-name services
- Google AdMob and related Google advertising services in Weather From Hell Free

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Euphoria Creations does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party service providers.

## Data Sharing

Euphoria Creations does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party service providers when necessary to provide application functionality, such as retrieving weather information or serving advertisements in Weather From Hell Free.

Third-party advertising services may collect and share data as described in their own documentation and privacy policies.

## Data Security

Euphoria Creations takes reasonable measures to limit access to user information to what is necessary for the operation of Weather From Hell.

Network communications with third-party services are intended to use secure encrypted connections where supported by those services.

Google states that user data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Weather From Hell does not currently operate a user account system or Euphoria Creations-hosted user profile database.

Application preferences, achievement information, notification settings, and similar application state are primarily stored locally on the user's device.

Users can remove locally stored Weather From Hell data by clearing the application's storage through Android settings or by uninstalling the application.

Information processed independently by third-party services may be retained according to the privacy and retention policies of those services.

## Children's Privacy

Weather From Hell is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

Euphoria Creations does not knowingly operate Weather From Hell for the purpose of collecting personal information from children.

Advertising provided in Weather From Hell Free is subject to Google's advertising policies and the application's configured audience and privacy settings.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Weather From Hell's functionality, third-party services, legal requirements, or privacy practices change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
