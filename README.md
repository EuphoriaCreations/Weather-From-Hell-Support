🔥 Weather From Hell

**Official Support & Documentation**

Weather From Hell is a weather app from **Euphoria Creations** featuring
Sulpher and the chronically dysfunctional Infernal Weather Department.

The weather may be accurate. The commentary is another matter entirely.

🛠️ Support

Having a problem with Weather From Hell?

You can report bugs and technical issues using the **Issues** section of
this repository.

When reporting an issue, please include:

- Your Android version
- Your Weather From Hell version
- A description of the problem
- Steps to reproduce the problem, if known
- Screenshots, if helpful

Please **do not post personal or sensitive information**, including your
exact GPS coordinates, in a public issue.

🌩️ Weather Information

Weather From Hell obtains weather information from third-party weather
data providers.

Weather conditions can change rapidly, and no weather application should
be used as the sole source of information during dangerous or severe
weather events.

Always follow official emergency alerts and instructions from local
authorities.

🔒 Privacy

Weather From Hell may request location access to provide weather
information for the user's current location.

A complete Privacy Policy will be available here prior to the public
release.
